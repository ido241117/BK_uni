Semester timeline & bead family

A: the Gợi ý lộ trình timeline, semester checkpoints as sparkles on a thread that shows progress. B: the bead family, one ring per course whose shape carries its state, used on every page. App: SemesterBoard.tsx, components/ui/Bead.tsx.

A. Semester timeline

The thread is solid where it fades in from the past, dashed for the plan, and ends at the graduation semester. Each checkpoint shows the credits accumulated after it. Empty semesters after graduation are hidden (they come back while a course is being moved, as targets).

B. Bead family

Every course is a ring (a bead with a hole, on the curriculum's thread). The state lives in the ring's shape; color only reinforces it, so it reads without color. A solid 6px pip is a flag on a row, never a state.

StateBeadWhat the shape says
Chưa khaiFaint dotted ring: no information yet
Chưa họcEmpty ring: not threaded yet
Đã lên kế hoạchDashed ring: intended, like the planned part of the thread
Đang họcHalf filled: halfway there
Đã hoàn thànhFilled bead with a pinhole: threaded
Chưa đạtSlashed ring: broken, to be retaken
