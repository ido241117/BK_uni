Controls

Buttons, inputs, dropdown, search, switch, checkbox, chips and tabs. App: components/Button, styles/antd-skin.css, components/ui/Controls.tsx. Focus is always a 2px --focus-ring outline (press Tab).

Buttons

One primary (rose stamp) per area. Stamps have a 4px radius and an inset hairline; hover is one step more emphatic. Danger actions confirm first.

Inputs and dropdown

Every input is underlined: an --ink-soft line, rose with a soft glow on focus, terracotta on error. Labels are 11px uppercase; hints sit below.

Search

SearchLine: magnifier, × to clear, optional / shortcut hint; the tinted variant is for dense toolbars.

Switch and checkbox

The switch is a bead on a thread: off = an --ink-soft ring, on = a filled rose bead with a glow. LabeledSwitch for inline filters, ToggleRow for settings. Checkbox and radio keep their native shape with a 3:1 edge.

Toggle rows (settings)

Chips and tabs

Chips (Segmented) switch views or filters inside a section: separate chips, hairline edge, selected = sparkle edge + blush + rose. Tabs (PageTabs) switch the sections of a page; the bar hides when there is one section.
